Veritra — Chain Link V identity

Concept 06 developed into a mark family, on the K2 · Bone palette. Two interlocked links draw the V: an end-to-end connection between exactly two parties, with the receiver's link passing over and the sender's tucking under it, its tip visible through the loop. The direction spends no accent hue, so the two links are one colour and the ground carries the identity.

Primary mark

Flat two-link mark with a keyline tuck-under. Deep plum on paper, bone on plum.

Monochrome

Single-ink variant for print, engraving, and one-colour contexts. The keyline gap carries the interlock.

App icon

Mark at 82% on a plum squircle. Fits iOS and Android adaptive-icon safe zones.

Favicon / small sizes

Simplified variant: heavier strokes, no keyline gap — it vanishes below ~24 px. It carries its own ground, because a hueless mark on a transparent field is invisible on one tab colour or the other.

Palette

Every value is a token from docs/design.md §K, not a brand colour of its own — which is what keeps green, amber, red and blue free to mean verified, warning, error and info.
