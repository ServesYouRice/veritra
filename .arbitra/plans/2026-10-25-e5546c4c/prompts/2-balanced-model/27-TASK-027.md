<goal>
Stop _refreshConversations and refreshCommunities in mobile/lib/core/app_state.dart from publishing, pruning drafts or persisting results that belong to a session other than the one that started the refresh. Apply the same guard to refreshInvites if it has the same pattern.

This matters because it fixes:
- Successful refreshes can publish data into a replacement session (high)
  These refreshes capture a session before awaiting network operations but do not validate ownership before publishing successful results. Public refreshes are not serialized through the session-transition queue. The conversation refresh additionally prunes composer drafts and persists the resulting projection.
