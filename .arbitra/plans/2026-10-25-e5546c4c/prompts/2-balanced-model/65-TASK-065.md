<goal>
In mobile/lib/features/chat/attachment_views.dart _AttachmentImageScreenState, check the image's declared dimensions before decoding, refuse images over a pixel cap, and decode allowed images at a bounded size.

This matters because it fixes:
- Peer-supplied image bytes are decoded at full intrinsic resolution (no cacheWidth/cacheHeight), enabling a decompression-bomb crash (medium)
  AttachmentImageScreen passes decrypted, sender-controlled bytes straight to Image.memory without cacheWidth or cacheHeight and without any pre-check of the declared dimensions. Flutter decodes images at their full declared size, so a small compressed file declaring an enormous pixel count can allocate gigabytes.
