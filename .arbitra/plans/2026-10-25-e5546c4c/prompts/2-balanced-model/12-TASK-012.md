<goal>
Store FCM token and UnifiedPush endpoint rotations durably in a generation-numbered pending record. AppState pulls the record once its push handling is ready and re-registers it with the server. The record is cleared only after that registration succeeds, so no rotation is lost to the listenerless Dart broadcast stream or to an early native listener attach.

This matters because it fixes:
- Endpoint rotations are discarded when Flutter is not listening (high)
  Both endpoint callbacks only emit an in-memory event. When Android starts a messaging service without a Flutter engine/listener, the nullable sink silently discards the replacement endpoint and its keys. Unlike wake events, endpoint updates have no durable pending record or background delivery mechanism in the supplied implementation. A later foreground registration may recover, but cannot maintain delivery while the app remains closed.
</goal>
