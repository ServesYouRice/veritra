<goal>
Ensure library-owned buffers and intermediate sensitive copies in crypto/rust/src/ffi.rs are wiped in a way the optimizer cannot remove, matching the veritra_crypto.h promise that 'freeing zeroes its contents'.

This matters because it fixes:
- pm_crypto_buffer_free zeroing is an eliminable dead store and intermediate plaintext copies are never wiped (medium)
  The header promises that freeing zeroes buffer contents. The implementation has three gaps: - buffer_free writes zeros with a plain fill(0) and then drops the Box immediately. Ordinary stores to memory that is deallocated right afterwards are eligible for dead-store elimination, because rustc marks the global deallocator as a free function for LLVM. The wipe can therefore vanish in optimized builds. - output() calls into_boxed_slice(), which reallocates whenever capacity exceeds length. The original buffer, holding plaintext, signatures or sealed state, is freed without being wiped. - Error paths drop plaintext Vecs without wiping them, for example when output() rejects its target after a successful decryption.
</goal>
