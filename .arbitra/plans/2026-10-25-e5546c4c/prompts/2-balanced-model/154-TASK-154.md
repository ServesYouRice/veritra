<goal>
Replace the shared 1024-slot w.downloads channel and its four runDownloads workers in server/cmd/veritra-load/workload.go with per-client-device download handling. Register each download with the recorder's in-flight registry (TASK-152) at enqueue, using the realtime event time as start. Settle each registration exactly once: completed when drained and fetched, dropped when the queue is full, or unfinished when loadCtx is cancelled.

This matters because it fixes:
- Attachment downloads use a fixed pool of 4 workers and are silently dropped when the queue is full (medium)
  The workload model says every online recipient downloads each attachment. In the code, runSocket queues downloads on a 1024-slot channel with a non-blocking send, and the default branch discards the job without recording anything. Only four goroutines drain that queue for all online devices and companion sockets. When the server slows, or 25 MiB attachments occupy the workers, the queue fills and later downloads vanish from both the offered load and the statistics. Download concurrency is also capped at 4 no matter how many devices are online.
</goal>
