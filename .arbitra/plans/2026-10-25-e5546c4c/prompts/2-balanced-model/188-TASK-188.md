<goal>
In server/internal/realtime/hub.go, derive the per-IP bucket key used by Hub.Register and removeLocked from remoteIP: a /64 prefix for IPv6 and the full address for IPv4 or IPv4-mapped addresses. An IPv6 client can then no longer rotate addresses inside its prefix to escape maxConnectionsPerIP. The IPv4 cap stays explicit pending section-dc6acc39e79b6ef9aaada424/Q-003.

This matters because it fixes:
- Realtime per-IP cap of 20 is keyed on the raw remote address string (medium)
  The hub limits sockets per exact remoteIP string to 20, across all accounts. For IPv4 behind carrier-grade or corporate NAT, many legitimate users share one address and exhaust the cap. For IPv6, a single client controls a /64 or larger prefix and can rotate addresses to bypass the per-IP limit entirely.
</goal>
