<goal>

- stage blobs in an invocation-owned, marked directory under filepath.Dir(StoragePath);
- check free space there;
- syncDir that parent after the blob preserve and activate renames, before writing the 'activated' journal, and during rollback.

This matters because it fixes:
- Restore commits without syncing a separately located blob directory's parent (high)

Recovery trusts the activated state and does not repair the resulting database/blob mismatch.
