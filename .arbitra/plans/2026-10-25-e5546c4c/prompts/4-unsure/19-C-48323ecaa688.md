<claim>
Welcome group-ID validation occurs after the stateful join (medium)

The expected group ID is checked only after StagedWelcome::new_from_welcome and into_group have completed. OpenMLS Welcome processing consumes one-time joining material, and into_group persists the joined group. Consequently, a valid Welcome for the wrong expected conversation can return InvalidMessage after changing provider state. The caller receives no group handle despite the completed join, and retrying the Welcome with its correct conversation ID may fail because its KeyPackage material has already been consumed.
</claim>
