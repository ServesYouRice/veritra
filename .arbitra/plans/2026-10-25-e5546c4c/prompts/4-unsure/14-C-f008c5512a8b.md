<claim>
Font-change handling dereferences the controller outside its lifetime guard (medium)

The controller guard protects plugin message dispatch but not the subsequent WM_FONTCHANGE branch. The native window exists before OnCreate initializes the controller, and Destroy calls OnDestroy before destroying the native window. A font-change message delivered during either lifetime gap therefore reaches an unconditional null dereference.
</claim>
